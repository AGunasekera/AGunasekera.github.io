Goals

Broad ambitions

  1. Accurate, size-consistent ground-state quantum chemistry across regimes of weak and strong correlation
  2. Understanding as deeply as possible (for myself, in the first instance) the correspondence between physical excited states and higher energy solutions to the Schr{\"o}dinger equation in finite basis sets
  3. Techniques for numerical convergence in coupled cluster theory

For each, a question that interests me

  1. What does it mean for two open-shell systems to be "non-interacting" when they are spin-coupled? Does size-consistency make sense with entanglement?
  2. Within the Hilbert space spanned by a discrete basis set that is designed for ground state molecules, to what extent are the higher energy solutions, even in FCI, representative of the excited states in continuous real space?
  3. Where do homotopy methods improve on perturbative quasi-Newton and DIIS? Is there something they do well for open-shell and strongly correlated systems?
